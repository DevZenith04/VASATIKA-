We Are a DSA —
Your Free Loan Matchmaker

Vasatika is a registered Direct Selling Agent (DSA) working with 9 leading banks and housing finance companies across Uttar Pradesh. We are not a bank — we are your expert guide who compares all lenders and gets you the best deal.

When you come to Vasatika, we understand your income, credit profile, property type, and goals — then match you with the bank that gives you the best rate and highest chance of approval.

"We've helped 500+ UP families get home loans — including many who were turned away directly by banks."

Our strength is knowing which bank to approach for which customer. A daily-wage earner in Varanasi needs a different lender than a software engineer in Lucknow. We know exactly who to call.
