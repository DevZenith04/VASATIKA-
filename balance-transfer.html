Practical field notes

A transfer is a comparison exercise, not a headline-rate chase.

Compare the remaining principal, current rate and reset history, time left, foreclosure or transfer charges, new processing costs, and the value of any top-up before deciding whether a switch is useful.

Current

Know the loan today

Collect outstanding balance, EMI, rate type, tenure remaining, and recent statements.

New

Price the switch

Model the new EMI, total interest, processing, legal, valuation, and other documented costs.

Decision

Compare the difference

A lower rate may not create value if the remaining tenure is short or the switching cost is high.
