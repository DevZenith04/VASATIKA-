Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common home loan questions from UP home buyers.

What is the current home loan interest rate in UP?
Home loan rates in UP range from 8.35% to 10.50% depending on the bank and your profile. SBI offers the lowest starting rate at 8.35% p.a. Rates depend on your CIBIL score, employment type, loan amount, and tenure. Vasatika compares all banks to find your best rate — completely free.
Can I get a home loan without a salary slip?
Yes! Banks like LIC Housing Finance, Aadhar Housing Finance, Shubham Housing, and Piramal offer home loans to self-employed, daily wage earners, and people without formal income proof. Vasatika specialises in these cases — contact us and we'll match you with the right lender.
How much home loan can I get on ₹30,000 monthly salary?
On a ₹30,000 monthly net salary you can typically get ₹18–22 lakhs depending on your existing EMIs. Banks generally allow 40–50% of net income as EMI. Use our eligibility calculator above or contact us — we'll calculate the exact amount for free.
How long does home loan approval take?
With Vasatika's assistance, most home loans are approved in 3–7 working days. HDFC Bank is fastest at 48 hours for salaried applicants. SBI may take 7–15 days. We pre-check your documents to avoid delays and rejections.
Is Vasatika's service free? How do you earn?
Yes, 100% free for customers. Vasatika is a registered Direct Selling Agent (DSA). When your loan is approved, the bank pays us a small commission. You pay the exact same rate as going directly to the bank — no extra charges from us whatsoever.
Which bank is best for home loan in Lucknow?
For salaried employees, SBI (8.35%) and HDFC (8.40%) are the best options in Lucknow. For self-employed, LIC Housing and PNB Housing offer flexible documentation. For those without income proof, Aadhar or Shubham are good choices. Contact Vasatika for a free personalised recommendation.
What is a DSA (Direct Selling Agent)?
A DSA is a registered loan facilitator who works with multiple banks. Vasatika is a DSA — we help you identify the best bank for your profile, prepare documents, and submit your application. We are paid by banks, not customers. Think of us as your free personal loan advisor.
