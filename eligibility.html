Practical field notes

Eligibility is a range to investigate, not a promise to quote.

An indicative result helps frame the next conversation. Final decisions can depend on lender policy, income proof, obligations, credit history, property assessment, age, location, and documentation.

Income

Show consistency

Salary slips, business records, bank credits, tax documents, and employment continuity can help the lender understand the income story.

Obligations

List the full picture
