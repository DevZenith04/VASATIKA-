Is eligibility the same as approval?

No. Eligibility is an indicative planning range. The lender makes the final decision after reviewing income, obligations, credit profile, property, valuation, legal checks, and documents.

What should I compare besides the interest rate?

Compare rate type, effective date, processing and legal costs, valuation, insurance, reset conditions, prepayment terms, tenure, and property policy.

Which documents are usually useful first?

Identity, address, income, bank, employment or business, existing-obligation, and property records are common starting points. The exact requirement depends on the route and lender.

Can I use WhatsApp for the first conversation?

Yes. Use the provided WhatsApp action and include your enquiry reference when you have one. Do not send sensitive documents until a secure production channel is confirmed.
