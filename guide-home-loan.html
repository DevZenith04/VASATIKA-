01 / Introduction

A home loan is a long-term commitment.

Securing a home loan is often the most significant financial decision a family makes. This guide breaks down the complexity into manageable pieces.

What is a home loan?

A home loan is a secured loan provided by a bank or housing finance company (HFC) to purchase, build, or renovate a residential property. The property itself serves as collateral until the loan is fully repaid.

Types of home loans

Lenders offer various routes depending on the property and purpose:

  • Home Purchase Loan: For buying a new or resale house/apartment.
  • Construction Loan: For building a home on a plot you already own.
  • Plot Loan: For purchasing a residential plot of land.
  • Balance Transfer: For switching an existing loan to a new lender for better terms.
  • Top-Up Loan: Additional funding on top of an existing home loan.

02 / Eligibility

How lenders assess your profile.

Lenders look at your ability and willingness to repay. The assessment is a combination of your financial profile and the property's legal/technical status.

Income & FOIR

Lenders use the Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR) to determine how much of your monthly income can go toward EMIs. This typically ranges from 40% to 65% depending on income tiers.

Credit Score

A CIBIL score of 750 or above is generally preferred for the best rates. Lower scores may lead to higher interest rates or rejection.

Age & Tenure

Most lenders require the loan to be repaid by the time the borrower reaches 60-70 years of age. Maximum tenure is usually 30 years.

03 / Process

From enquiry to disbursement.

The journey involves multiple stages of verification. Understanding the sequence helps you prepare the right documents at the right time.

02

03

Legal & Technical

04

Disbursement
