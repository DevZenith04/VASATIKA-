Practical field notes

A home loan is a long-term monthly decision.

The purchase price is only one part of the picture. Review the contribution, repayment range, property paperwork, lender conditions, and timeline before treating a quote as a plan.

Budget

Contribution and comfort

Separate the amount you may be eligible for from the amount that keeps the monthly budget comfortable.

File

Property readiness

Agreement, title, approvals, valuation, seller or developer records, and address details may matter to the lender review.

Conversation

Questions to carry

Ask about rate type, reset terms, processing costs, legal and valuation fees, insurance, and prepayment conditions.
