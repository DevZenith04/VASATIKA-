Eligibility & Documents

What to prepare

Indian Bank requires a clear income and property trail. Keep these basics ready for a first review:

01

KYC Records

PAN, Aadhaar, and verified address proof.

02

Income Proof

Salary slips (3m), Bank statements (6m), and ITR.

03

Property File

Approved plans, title deeds, and tax receipts.

Why borrowers pick Indian Bank

Public sector trust.

Borrowers often choose Indian Bank for its transparent public-sector processing, competitive repo-linked rates, and the security of a long-standing national banking institution.
