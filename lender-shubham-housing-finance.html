What to prepare

Shubham considers informal income. Keep these basics ready for a first review:

01

Identity Proof

PAN, Aadhaar, and current address proof.

02

Income Context

Business records, bank statements, or income self-declaration.

03

Property Records

Title deeds, allotment letters, and local approvals.

Why borrowers pick Shubham

Inclusive financing.

Borrowers often choose Shubham Housing Finance for its inclusive approach to credit, its ability to assess informal income streams, and its commitment to helping families who are excluded by traditional banking systems.
