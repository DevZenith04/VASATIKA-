All Housing Loan Types

We arrange home loans from 9 bank partners for every housing need. Compare and apply — free.

Home Loan — Purchase

Rate: 8.35%–10.35% p.a.

Buy a ready-to-move, under-construction, or resale property. Up to 90% of property value for salaried and self-employed.

  • Salaried: 3 months salary slip + Form 16
  • Self-employed: 2 years ITR + bank statement
  • No income proof via LIC, Shubham, Aadhar

Home Construction Loan

Rate: 8.40%–11.00% p.a.

Own a plot? Build your dream home. Disbursed in stages linked to construction progress — pay interest only on amount drawn.

  • Land ownership proof required
  • Approved building plan needed
  • Disbursed in 4–6 stages

Balance Transfer + Top-Up

Rate: 8.35%–9.95% p.a.

Move your existing home loan from a high-rate bank to a better lender. Save lakhs in interest. Get top-up funds for extra needs.

  • Minimum 12 months repayment track needed
  • No foreclosure charges from most lenders
  • Can reduce EMI by ₹2,000–8,000/month

Loan Against Property (LAP)

Rate: 9.00%–14.00% p.a.

Mortgage your residential or commercial property for business, medical, education, or personal needs. Up to 65–70% of market value.

  • Residential & commercial both accepted
  • Long tenure, lower EMI
  • Piramal specialises in LAP

  • Title deed / registry papers
