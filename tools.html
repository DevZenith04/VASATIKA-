01 / Rent vs. buy

Compare the cost of keeping flexibility with the cost of building ownership.

Buying combines the down payment, loan repayment, and ownership costs with a possible property value. Renting keeps capital invested but has escalation. This model is a planning estimate, not financial advice.

Assumptions are simplified. Add maintenance, taxes, transaction costs, vacancy, tax treatment, and actual investment returns before making a decision.

Affordability should rise with income, but not without a ceiling.

Separate the property stage before you calculate the tax.
